# Data credits

- **Quranic Arabic Corpus**, morphology v0.4 - Kais Dukes, University of Leeds. https://corpus.quran.com
  Licensed under the GNU General Public License. Attribution required.
- **quran-morphology** - mustafa0x, a cleaned fork of the above. https://github.com/mustafa0x/quran-morphology
- **Tanzil Quran Text** and the Sahih International translation - https://tanzil.net
  The text must not be modified; it is only filtered and re-arranged in code, never edited.

The files in `data/raw/` are the originals, unchanged. Manual corrections live in `data/corrections.csv`.
